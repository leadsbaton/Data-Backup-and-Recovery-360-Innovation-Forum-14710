Data Backup & Recovery 360 Innovation Forum

The Data Backup & Recovery 360 Innovation Forum is the place to be when it comes to modernizing enterprise data resilience to overcome hidden IT risks, ransomware, and compliance challenges. The forum highlight AI-powered protection, endpoint security, and strategies to ensure swift recovery from episodes affecting the industry. Key themes include: Modernizing Data Resilience: Moving from basic backups to comprehensive, automated recovery strategies. Proactive Defense: Integrating AI into backup systems to detect threats in real time. Endpoint Security: Protecting data for remote and hybrid workforces and Risk Mitigation: Addressing hidden vulnerabilities in IT infrastructure before they cause disruption.
